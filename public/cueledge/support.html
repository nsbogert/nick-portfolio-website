Support

CueLedge is a teleprompter for Mac that follows your voice as you read.

Email: cueledge@nickbogert.com

I usually reply within two business days. Tell me what you expected and what happened instead. If it's about Voice mode, choose Help › Copy Diagnostics in CueLedge and paste the result into your email: it shows app events and error codes, never your script.

Requirements

A Mac with Apple silicon (an M-series chip) running macOS 15 Sequoia or later. Voice mode understands US English.

Common questions

The script isn't following my voice

  • Check the microphone. In CueLedge › Settings › Microphone, choose your mic under Listen with. Bluetooth headsets often sound muffled to speech recognition; your Mac's built-in mic or a USB mic works better.
  • Check permissions. In System Settings › Privacy & Security, CueLedge needs to be turned on under both Microphone and Speech Recognition.
  • Turn on Dictation. CueLedge recognizes speech on your Mac only, which needs Apple's on-device speech model. In System Settings › Keyboard, turn on Dictation and set its language to English (US). While connected to the internet, wait a few minutes for macOS to download the speech model, then try Help › Test Your Voice in CueLedge.
  • Lost your place? Scroll the prompter to the line you're on, and CueLedge follows you from there.

I'd rather not use the microphone

Use Steady mode. It scrolls at a pace you set and needs no permissions.

Where are my scripts stored?

On your Mac, and in any backups of it, like Time Machine. CueLedge never uploads them. To keep a copy or move a script to another Mac, open it and choose File › Export.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose I'd like to › Request a refund, then select CueLedge. Apple decides refunds.

How is my privacy protected?

CueLedge never sends your scripts or your voice anywhere, and it has no accounts, ads or analytics. Read the privacy policy.
