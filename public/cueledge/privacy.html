Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

In short: The CueLedge app doesn't collect your data. Your scripts, your voice and your settings stay on your Mac. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. If you email me for support, I get what you choose to send, and I use it only to help you.

CueLedge is a teleprompter app for Mac, made by Nicholas Bogert, an independent developer in Seattle, Washington ("I" and "me" below). This policy covers the CueLedge app and these support pages.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your scripts, and where you left off in each one, are saved in CueLedge's private folder on your Mac. They aren't uploaded anywhere.
  • Your settings, such as colors, speed and keyboard shortcuts, are saved on your Mac.
  • Files you import or export are read or written only where you choose, when you choose.

CueLedge itself can't connect to the internet, and I have no server that receives anything from it. macOS services it relies on, like the App Store for purchases and the download of Apple's speech model, connect on Apple's terms.

Your microphone and speech

Voice mode scrolls the script as you read it aloud. To do that, CueLedge asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition.

  • The microphone is on only while Voice mode is listening or you're running Help › Test Your Voice. macOS shows its microphone indicator whenever it's on.
  • CueLedge tells macOS to recognize speech on your Mac only. If on-device recognition isn't available, Voice mode doesn't start, rather than sending your voice anywhere.
  • CueLedge uses audio and recognized words only in the moment to keep your place, and never saves or logs them.

macOS's permission prompt may mention that speech can be sent to Apple. That's Apple's general wording for all apps; CueLedge doesn't use that option. Steady mode, which scrolls at a set pace, needs neither permission.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. If you buy the unlock, Apple gives the app a record of the purchase (what you bought and when) so it can turn on the features. That record never includes your name, Apple Account or payment details, and it stays on your Mac. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Crash reports and TestFlight

If you've chosen in your Mac's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send me crash reports and usage statistics. Apple shares these with me without your name or Apple Account. If you test a pre-release version through TestFlight, Apple shares the feedback and screenshots you choose to send, along with your TestFlight name and email, under Apple's Privacy Policy. I use these only to fix problems.

Support email

If you email cueledge@nickbogert.com, I receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. That address forwards through Cloudflare to my Google mail account, so those companies handle the message in transit and storage under their own privacy policies. They act as my email providers, use it only to deliver and store my mail, and protect it at least as well as this policy describes.

  • Diagnostics: Help › Copy Diagnostics copies a short report: app and macOS versions, your Mac's chip type, and recent app events with their times and error codes. Help › Copy Take Report works the same way, with counts and timings only. Neither ever contains your script or anything you said. It's sent only if you paste it into an email yourself.
  • I use your email only to answer you and to fix the problem you reported. I don't add you to a mailing list, and I never sell or share your email.
  • I delete support email within two years of our last message, or sooner if you ask.

Children

The CueLedge app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If a child emails me, I use the message only to reply, and I delete it as soon as I've answered, or sooner if a parent asks.

Your choices

  • You can turn off microphone or speech access anytime in System Settings › Privacy & Security, and use Steady mode instead.
  • Deleting only the app leaves your scripts in place, in case you reinstall. To remove everything CueLedge stored, quit it, delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.cueledge.app, then delete the app.
  • You can ask me to see or delete any email you've sent. Write to the address above.

If you're in the EU or UK: I'm the controller of support email. I process it because you asked for help, which is my legitimate interest in answering you. It's stored in the US by Google. You can ask me to access, correct or delete it, and you can complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here with a new effective date. If a change means CueLedge starts collecting data, I'll say so in the app's release notes and update the App Store privacy label before it ships.
